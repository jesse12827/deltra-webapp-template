# User guide

Replace this guide with the application's own user and administrator guide
(Web-App Development Stack, Section 19). The template's example application works as follows.

## Signing in
Choose **Sign in with Microsoft** and use your Deltra work account. If you see "Access denied",
an administrator has not yet added you, or your account is inactive.

## Items (example)
- **Dashboard** shows how many items are in each status. Select a status to see those items.
- **Items** lists all items. Search by title, filter by status, sort by title or due date, and
  choose 25, 50, 75, or 100 per page. The address bar keeps your search, so you can bookmark it.
- **New item** creates an item. Title is required.
- If someone else changed an item while you were editing it, your save is refused with a message.
  Reload the item and make your change again.

## Administrators
**Admin** shows the audit log: sign-ins, denied access, and every change, with who made it and when.
